<h1 align="center">PETAL & CRUMB</h1>

<p align="center"><i>sweet moments, beautifully baked.</i></p>

<br>

A little bakery brought to life on the web.

Petal & Crumb is a soft and elegant bakery webpage created around the idea of making a bakery feel just as warm and inviting online as it would in person. The page brings together delicate desserts, the story behind the bakery, a popular menu, booking details, and a way to get in touch — all within one consistent visual style.

The design leans into soft pinks, dessert photography, clean spacing, and simple layouts to keep the page feeling light, warm, and a little dreamy.
