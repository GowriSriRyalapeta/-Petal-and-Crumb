Our Story

Petal & Crumb began with a simple love for baking and a dream of turning everyday moments into something a little sweeter. What started as a tiny kitchen filled with the aroma of fresh cakes and pastries slowly blossomed into a bakery made for life's beautiful moments.

Every creation is thoughtfully baked, beautifully finished, and made with the belief that the sweetest memories are always worth sharing.
